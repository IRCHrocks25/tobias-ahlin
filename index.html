About Tobias

You already know what takes the most time. I build what removes it.

Dörröppnaren isn't a metaphor I picked for a website. It's what I actually did.

Twenty years ago I started selling to the world's most demanding data companies. Betting operators, gaming platforms, banks. In the last ten years of it alone, I sold over 30 million dollars of the most expensive software there is and made President's Club five times. At Snowflake, my last stop, I watched the company grow from 350 million dollars to 2.6 billion while I was there.

Somewhere in there my body started failing before I noticed. Bad kidneys, a bad liver, high cholesterol. All from stress. In the same stretch of years I lost nine people close to me to cancer.

My wife's youngest sister. A colleague I'd worked beside for years. My stepbrother.

Nine people. I had the money to stop. I didn't know how much time I had left to use it.

Behind the numbers, the picture was different. Constant pressure, never enough, and children growing up without me noticing. A blood test at 42 showed what my body thought of the pace: high cholesterol, poor kidney and liver values.

So in April 2023 I walked away. Not fired, not burned out into a settlement. I chose it, on my own terms, at 42.

The name of this company is Dörröppnaren. The Door Opener. I closed one very large door on my old life, and I've been opening smaller, better ones ever since. Now I open them for other people too.

I don't sell tools. I build the operational system that lets an owner-led company grow without the owner being the bottleneck. That's the whole job.
